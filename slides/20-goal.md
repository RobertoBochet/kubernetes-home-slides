## Talk's Goals

<v-clicks>

**Everybody**: a clear idea of what Kubernetes is and what it does

**Every self-hoster**: an idea of how Kubernetes could be brought home

**At least one in ten**: the wish to dig into Kubernetes, even if only in theory

**Even one person**: taking Kubernetes home<sup>*</sup>, and the talk has done its job

</v-clicks>

<p class="color-secondary text-sm mt-10" v-click="4">
<sup>*</sup><strong>Home</strong> means self-hosting: a home server, or a rented VPS
</p>
